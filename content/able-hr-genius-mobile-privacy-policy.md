## Introduction

Welcome to Able HR Genius Mobile.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information. By using our app, you agree to the terms outlined below.

## Information We Collect

**Camera Access:** Our app uses your device's camera to scan QR codes.

**Location Data:** When you scan a QR code and you press the login button or the logout button, we collect your location data to provide relevant information.

## How We Use Your Data

We use the collected data to:

- **Scan QR Codes:** To provide the requested information.
- **Improve Our Services:** To enhance user experience and app functionality.

## Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes mentioned above.

## User Consent

By using our app, you consent to the collection and use of your data as described in this Privacy Policy.
